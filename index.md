Art, Technology, Architecture (@A) is a research group part of the <a href="https://cma.hkust-gz.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Computational Media and Arts Thrust</a> at the <a href="https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (GZ)</a>. 

We are an <a href="https://doi.org/10.7238/artnodes.v0i39.980000018781" target="_blank" rel="noopener">un-disciplinary</a> collective conducting research on the impact and effects of emerging technologies on creative practices with a focus on architecture and design. We make use of computational design and digital fabrication, and collaborate with AI systems and living materials to create speculative concepts, artworks, and build architectural demonstrators. We focus on both the practical and the theoretical aspects of the impact of novel tools and materials on artistic and designerly outputs in the context of designing (for) sustainable futures.
